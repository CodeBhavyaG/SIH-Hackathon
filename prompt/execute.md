# Research Brief

## Research Question
What are the multifaceted socio-economic implications of artificial intelligence (AI) on the global job market?

## Objective
Analyze both job displacement due to automation and the creation of new roles and industries. Examine how AI is reshaping skill requirements and assess the need for workforce reskilling and upskilling initiatives.

## Scope
- Potential increases in income inequality
- The rise of the gig economy driven by AI platforms
- Ethical considerations around algorithmic bias in hiring and performance evaluations
- Case studies from manufacturing, customer service, and healthcare
- Policy interventions such as universal basic income and adaptive education systems

## Expected Deliverable
A comprehensive research report explaining how AI is changing employment, skills, income distribution, workplace ethics, and industry structures. The report should include relevant industry case studies and practical policy recommendations to manage the transition and ensure equitable benefits from AI-driven productivity gains.
